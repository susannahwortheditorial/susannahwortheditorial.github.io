I’m a skilled and creative freelance editor with over 15 years’ experience helping artists, academics, writers, designers and cultural institutions express themselves clearly, confidently and in their own voice.

Who I Work With

I work with clients across the arts and cultural fields — especially those writing about visual art, performance, architecture and design. My background in critical writing and art history helps me support your ideas without flattening your voice or tone.

Whether you’re a museum or gallery producing interpretation texts, an academic in the arts and humanities preparing a thesis, or an artist, curator or designer refining a statement or catalogue, I bring care, curiosity and precision to every collaboration.

How I Can Help

  • Editing and proofreading for art books, exhibition catalogues, websites and press materials
  • Support with PhD theses, academic essays and presentations
  • Finessing artist and curator statements, proposals and funding applications
  • Refining translations or supporting non-native English writers to find clarity and flow
  • Ensuring accessible and inclusive language in both digital and print formats

Special Expertise

Editing for Non-Native English Speakers

I have extensive experience supporting authors writing in English as a second language, helping refine both original texts and translations while retaining the author’s intent and tone.

Digital Production & CMS Editing

I’m confident working directly in content management systems like Drupal, Wagtail and Squarespace, with a strong understanding of best practices in editing for web — including accessibility, alt text and hyperlink structure.

Accessibility & Inclusive Language

I bring knowledge of accessibility principles to ensure your texts are inclusive, readable and effective across platforms and audiences — including screen reader–friendly formatting and meaningful image descriptions.

Additional Support for Writers

If you’re preparing for a viva, interview, talk, panel, book launch or any kind of public speaking, you might benefit from a different kind of support — one that goes beyond the written word, from page to presence.

Through my other business, Gutsy, I support shy, sensitive and socially anxious folks build the confidence to speak up and share their ideas — without changing their quiet, introvert nature. I use gentle, playful techniques from drama, improvisation and hypnotic visualisation to help you warm up, rehearse and connect to your voice with confidence.

About Me

I’m based in London and have a background in art history and critical writing. I studied History of Art at Edinburgh University and later completed an MA in Critical Writing in Art & Design at the Royal College of Art.

In addition to freelance editing, I’m a published author — my book Digesting Recipes was published by Zero Books in 2015 — and I’ve contributed to various gallery and editorial projects.

Whether on the page or in person, my work is about helping people find freedom in expression — nurturing clarity, liberating ideas and building confidence.
